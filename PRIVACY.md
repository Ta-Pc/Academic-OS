# Privacy Policy for Academic OS

**Last updated:** February 13, 2026

Academic OS ("we," "our," or "us") is an open-source academic performance tracking tool developed by Sipho Zuma. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web, mobile, and desktop applications (collectively, the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Google User Data
Academic OS uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate you and access your Google Drive. We request access to the following scope:
* `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` (or `drive.appdata`): This allows the application to save and retrieve your specific Academic OS database files (`.db` or `.json`) to synchronize your data across devices.

**We do not view, scrape, or store your personal Google Drive files unrelated to Academic OS.**

### B. Local Data
Your academic data (grades, modules, tasks) is stored locally on your device using SQLite (WASM) and synchronized strictly to your own personal Google Drive.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect solely to:
* Provide the cloud synchronization feature (backing up your academic data to your Google Drive).
* Resolve data conflicts between your devices (e.g., syncing changes from Android to Web).
* Generate performance analytics (graphs, transcript reports) within the app.

**We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties.** The developer (Sipho Zuma) has no access to your grades or personal files; they exist only on your device and your cloud storage.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
* **Retention:** Your data is retained as long as you keep the files in your Google Drive or local storage.
* **Deletion:** You can delete your data at any time by deleting the Academic OS files from your Google Drive or clearing your browser/app data.

## 4. Limited Use Policy
Academic OS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
